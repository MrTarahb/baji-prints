About.

I make photographs and print them. There's a gap between an image on a screen and an image on paper — in the weight of it, the texture, the light it holds. My work lives in that gap.

I work across ICM, abstract, street, and macro. Most of what I make is in black and white, though colour finds its way in when it earns it.

Based in Wiedikon, Zürich. I work from a small atelier with a fully colour-calibrated setup on an Epson SC-P900 — up to A2, across a range of fine art papers.

Privacy policy.

This page explains what information is collected on bharatbhatia.photography, why, and how it's handled. Last updated June 2026.

Contact form. If you fill in the contact form, I collect your name, email address, the category you select, and any message you write. This is stored so I can reply to you and is never shared with third parties. It's used only to respond to your enquiry.

Site analytics. When you visit this site, I log the page visited, the time, and the referring site (e.g. Instagram, Google) if your browser provides one. This is anonymous — no cookies, no IP address storage, no tracking across other websites. It's used only to understand roughly how many people visit and where from.

Admin session. A single session cookie is used only when I log into the admin panel to manage the site. It is never set for regular visitors.

Orders and payments. If you purchase a print, payment is processed entirely by Stripe — I never see or store your card details. Stripe's own privacy policy governs that data. I store your name, shipping address, and order details only to fulfil and ship your order.

Images. Photographs are hosted via Cloudinary, a third-party image hosting service. No personal data is attached to images.

Your rights. You can ask me to delete any data I hold about you — message me via the contact form or email directly. I don't sell or share your data with advertisers, ever.

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact page.

Terms of sale.

These terms apply to any print purchased through bharatbhatia.photography. Last updated June 2026.

Who you're buying from. Bharat Bhatia, operating as an individual print atelier based in Wiedikon, Zürich, Switzerland. For any question, get in touch via the contact page or email directly.

The contract. Placing an order and completing payment forms a binding sale, as is standard under Swiss law. You'll receive an order confirmation by email once payment is confirmed, along with a separate formal receipt from Stripe, our payment processor.

Pricing and payment. All prices are shown in Swiss francs (CHF) and include everything except the delivery fee, which is calculated and shown separately at checkout based on your chosen delivery method and print size. Payment is handled securely by Stripe; card details are never seen or stored by me.

Delivery. Swiss law doesn't set a maximum delivery period, but I aim to print, package, and ship every order within a reasonable time after payment, generally within one to two weeks unless otherwise noted. You'll get a confirmation email once your order ships. A4 prints are sent flat in a protective mailer; A3 and A2 prints are shipped rolled in a postal tube. Personal delivery (Zürich area) is arranged directly with you.

Returns. I want you to be genuinely happy with your print. You may return any print within 30 days of delivery, for any reason, no questions asked. This applies to orders shipped within Switzerland and the EU alike. To start a return, just get in touch with your order reference. You're responsible for the cost of return shipping; once the print arrives back in good condition, I'll refund the full purchase price (excluding the original delivery fee).

If you're an EU-based customer, this 30-day policy is more generous than the 14-day right of withdrawal required by EU law, so you're fully covered either way.

Damaged or faulty prints. If your print arrives damaged or there's a defect, contact me within a reasonable time of receiving it. In these cases, the standard 30-day return applies and I'll also cover the return shipping cost. Swiss law additionally provides a two-year statutory warranty against defects on new goods, which applies regardless of the return window above.

Limited editions. Where a print is sold as a limited edition, the edition size and your print's position within it are recorded at the time of sale and will not be exceeded.

Liability. I take care in packaging every print to prevent damage in transit. If a parcel is visibly damaged on arrival, please document it (photos help) and contact me promptly so we can sort it out together.

Governing law. These terms are governed by Swiss law. Any dispute will be handled under the jurisdiction of the courts of Zürich, Switzerland, without prejudice to any mandatory consumer protections that apply in your own country of residence.

This page is a good-faith summary, not formal legal advice. Questions about an order can be sent via the contact page.

      Prints & paper

      Each print is produced on museum-grade fine art paper — the specific paper for each image is noted in the shop. I use archival papers such as Hahnemühle Photo Rag and similar. The paper choice is made per image to best complement the photograph.
      Prints are available in A4, A3, and A2 — standard DIN paper sizes. A4 is approximately 21×30cm, A3 is 30×42cm, and A2 is 42×60cm. Not all sizes are available for every image; available sizes and prices are shown per print in the shop.
      Yes. All prints are signed by hand. Limited edition prints are also numbered.
      A limited edition means only a fixed number of that print will ever be produced in that size. The edition size is shown in the shop. Once the edition sells out, no more copies of that print in that size will be made. Your edition number is recorded at the time of purchase.
      Some limited editions are also offered through other platforms, such as Artfinder. The edition size is shared across all of them — if a print has an edition of 50, that's 50 total, not 50 per platform. Every sale, wherever it happens, is recorded against the same running number, so the edition limit is always honoured and no print is ever oversold. A print may also be available as an open edition on this site even if a limited edition of it is listed elsewhere — the two are always clearly distinguished, and only the limited version is signed, numbered, and capped.
      Yes — all prints are delivered with a white border, heavier at the bottom (classic passepartout proportions). If you'd prefer something different — equal margins, no margins, or a custom layout — just let me know in the notes field when ordering and I'll accommodate it.

      Ordering & payment

      Browse the shop, select a print and size, add it to your cart, choose a delivery method, and proceed to checkout. Payment is handled securely by Stripe — your card details are never seen or stored by me.
      Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and TWINT for Swiss customers. All payments are processed securely via Stripe.
      Yes — if you've seen a photo in my portfolio that isn't currently available as a print, just get in touch and I'll arrange it for you.

      Shipping

      A4 prints are sent flat in a rigid protective mailer. A3 and A2 prints are shipped rolled in a sturdy postal tube. All prints are carefully packaged to arrive in perfect condition.
      I aim to print and dispatch every order within one to two weeks of payment. Swiss delivery typically takes 2–4 working days after dispatch; EU and international delivery takes longer depending on destination. You'll receive a confirmation email when your order ships.
      Yes — I ship to EU countries and a range of other destinations worldwide. Shipping cost is calculated at checkout based on your country and the size of your order. For destinations not listed at checkout, contact me for a quote.
      Shipping is calculated automatically at checkout based on your delivery method, destination, and the size and weight of your prints. Swiss standard shipping starts from CHF 2 for A4 and CHF 9 for A3/A2. EU rates vary by weight — shown at checkout once you select your country.

      Personal delivery

      Personal delivery means I bring the print to you in person — no postal service, no tube. It's the most careful way to receive a large fine art print, and it gives us a chance to meet and make sure you're happy with the piece.
      Personal delivery is available across Switzerland and Liechtenstein. After placing your order, I'll contact you directly to arrange a time and location that works for you.
      Once your order is confirmed and the print is ready, I'll reach out by email to coordinate. We arrange a meeting point — your home, your office, or anywhere convenient. I bring the print in person, flat or rolled depending on size, and you can inspect it on the spot before I leave.

      Returns & quality

      If your print arrives damaged, contact me as soon as possible with photos of the damage and packaging. I'll arrange a replacement or full refund, including return shipping costs, at no charge to you.
      Yes — you can return any print within 30 days of delivery, for any reason, no questions asked. Return shipping is at your cost; once the print arrives back in good condition I'll refund the full purchase price. See the terms of sale for full details.

      Framing

      Not yet — but it's coming. I'm in the process of learning to build my own frames by hand. Once available, framing will be offered as an optional add-on at checkout. Get in touch if you'd like to know more or be notified when it launches.
