About.

I make photographs and print them. There's a gap between an image on a screen and an image on paper — in the weight of it, the texture, the light it holds. My work lives in that gap.

I work across ICM, abstract, street, and macro. Most of what I make is in black and white, though colour finds its way in when it earns it.

Based in Wiedikon, Zürich. I work from a small atelier with a fully colour-calibrated setup on an Epson SC-P900 — up to A2, across a range of fine art papers.

Epson SC-P900 · 10-channel pigment ink · Up to A2 · Photo Rag Pearl · Mono Silk Warmtone · Photo Rag Satin & more

Privacy policy.

This page explains what information is collected on bharatbhatia.photography, why, and how it's handled. Last updated June 2026.

Contact form. If you fill in the contact form, I collect your name, email address, the category you select, and any message you write. This is stored so I can reply to you and is never shared with third parties. It's used only to respond to your enquiry.

Site analytics. When you visit this site, I log the page visited, the time, and the referring site (e.g. Instagram, Google) if your browser provides one. This is anonymous — no cookies, no IP address storage, no tracking across other websites. It's used only to understand roughly how many people visit and where from.

Admin session. A single session cookie is used only when I log into the admin panel to manage the site. It is never set for regular visitors.

Orders and payments. If you purchase a print, payment is processed entirely by Stripe — I never see or store your card details. Stripe's own privacy policy governs that data. I store your name, shipping address, and order details only to fulfil and ship your order.

Images. Photographs are hosted via Cloudinary, a third-party image hosting service. No personal data is attached to images.

Your rights. You can ask me to delete any data I hold about you — message me via the contact form or email directly. I don't sell or share your data with advertisers, ever.

Questions about this policy can be sent via the contact page.
